3. DEEP-DIVE INTO THE NEW MODULES
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. "SCAN BEFORE YOU BUY" & PRICE-GOUGING RADAR (Consumer)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Consumer Scans Barcode in store:
   • Instant lookup (<100ms) returns:
     - Verified MRP: ₹45.00
     - Net Quantity: 100g (₹0.45/g)
     - Compliance Badge: 🥇 Gold (98% Trust Score)
     - Shelf-life / Expiry sanity check
     - Past violations count: 0

2. Price-Gouging / Tampering Detector:
   • Every consumer and officer scan logs: `(barcode, printed_mrp, store_gps, timestamp)`.
   • Anomaly Algorithm:
     - Group scans by `barcode` over a sliding 30-day window.
     - Compute modal (standard) MRP.
     - IF $printed\_mrp > modal\_mrp \times 1.05$ at specific retailer:
       → Auto-flag **"MRP Tampering / Overcharging Hotspot"**.
       → Alert consumer: *"⚠️ Warning: Standard MRP is ₹45. You are being charged ₹55."*
       → Auto-dispatch inspection lead to local officer queue!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. CITIZEN REPORTING & REPUTATION TRUST SCORE (Anti-Spam)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Citizen Submission:
   • User snaps photo of suspected violation (e.g. over-pasting sticker on MRP).
   • Selects category: Overcharging, Missing Expiry, Expired Product, No Net Qty.
   • Submits with GPS + Store Name.

2. Gamified Reporter Trust Score ($T_{rep} \in [0, 100]$):
   • New reporter starts at $T_{rep} = 50$.
   • When an officer inspects and confirms the violation: $T_{rep} \mathrel{+}= 10$.
   • If report is verified spam/false: $T_{rep} \mathrel{-}= 20$.
   • High-trust reporters ($T_{rep} > 80$) get "Verified Vigilant Citizen" badge, and
     their reports jump to the top of the officer's daily priority queue!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. PREDICTIVE ENFORCEMENT ROUTING (Officer Intelligence)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Instead of random patrols, the officer opens **"Today's Priority Route"**:
• Risk Score per Store/Area ($R_{store}$):
  $$R_{store} = w_1 \cdot \text{HistoricalViolations} + w_2 \cdot \text{CitizenLeads} + w_3 \cdot \text{PriceGougingAnomalies} + w_4 \cdot \text{SeasonalMultiplier}$$
  *(e.g., Sweet & Ghee shops get $2.5\times$ risk multiplier during Diwali week)*
• The app calculates the optimal patrol path connecting top 5 high-risk retail hotspots.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. AR LIVE OVERLAY IN CAMERA VIEWFINDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Using WebAssembly / TensorFlow.js / OpenCV on device:
• As officer pans camera over packaging, HUD highlights:
  - Green bounding box over detected MRP: *"MRP ₹120 ✓"*
  - Green box over Net Qty: *"Net Wt 500g ✓"*
  - Flashing red box on blank area: *"⚠️ Mfg Date Missing on this panel"*
• Gives instant feedback before pressing shutter!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. PEER-REVIEW & SENIOR ESCALATION WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• When a case has:
  - Confidence score between 0.60 and 0.75
  - Disputed unit price calculation
  - High penalty fine (> ₹50,000 / corporate repeat offender)
• Status set to: `STATUS_PENDING_PEER_REVIEW`.
• Routed to Senior Legal Metrology Officer dashboard.
• Senior officer reviews raw pixel crops, clicks "Approve Notice" or "Dismiss with Remarks".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F. BRAND PRE-MARKET SELF-CERTIFICATION SANDBOX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Brand packaging team uploads digital label artwork / die-line (PDF/PNG) prior to printing.
2. System runs 10-point compliance engine:
   - Font height ratio verification against target carton dimensions
   - Unit sale price formula validation
   - Mandatory declaration placement check
3. Result:
   - If compliant: Issues cryptographic **"Digital Compliance Certificate"** + verified QR.
   - If non-compliant: Generates actionable PDF: *"Fix Net Qty font (currently 1.6mm, needs 2.0mm)"*.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G. BRAND DISPUTE & APPEAL WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• When a violation notice is generated, brand receives email notification with secure token.
• Brand has 15 days to access the **Dispute Portal**:
  - View exact high-res pixel crops cited by the officer.
  - Submit rebuttal: attach manufacturing batch record, authorized lab test, or packaging variance notice.
  - Legal Metrology appellate officer reviews both evidence streams before court filing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H. E-COMMERCE VS PHYSICAL PACK CROSS-VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Crawler checks product listings on Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto.
• Cross-references digital product description vs verified physical pack:
  - Is MRP displayed on e-com equal to physical pack MRP? (Flags dark pattern markups)
  - Is country of origin disclosed on product listing? (Mandatory under E-Commerce LM Rules)
  - Is Net Quantity listed accurately?
• Flags e-commerce platform non-compliance under Rule 6(10) of Packaged Commodities Rules.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I. INTER-AGENCY UNIFIED REGULATORY GRAPH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Connects multiple Indian regulatory identifiers:
  - **Legal Metrology Scan** $\leftrightarrow$ **FSSAI License** $\leftrightarrow$ **BIS / ISI Mark** $\leftrightarrow$ **GSTIN**
• If a company commits repeat packaging fraud, the system automatically checks:
  - Is the FSSAI license active? (Flags invalid licenses to Food Safety authority)
  - Is the GSTIN active? (Flags shell companies to Tax enforcement)
• Macro-level dashboard allows Ministry of Consumer Affairs to visualize systemic non-compliance.
